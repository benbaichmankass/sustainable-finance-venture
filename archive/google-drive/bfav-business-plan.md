## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Business Concept

A project finance and management firm for solar energy generation that works with the agricultural sector to create added value from farmland. We focus on adding bifacial-agrivoltaic solar capacity that can be sold to the electric utility, on agricultural land that is utilized for appropriate crop rotations.

The firm is a sole proprietorship that collaborates with partners and subcontractors to finance and deliver solar energy projects.

### 1.2 Business Goals & Vision

Harnessing untapped value potential of farmland for solar energy to advance the green transition and create added value for farmers from their land assets.

### 1.3 Product description and differentiation. What do you sell, and why is it different?

Model — investment is derisked for landholder, making it much easier to move projects forward.

### 1.4 Target market. Who do you sell to?

Our end customer is the electric utility that purchases electricity generated from our projects. However, the business model is built on partnerships with farmers (and investors), while revenue is generated from relatively rigid PPAs in a monopsony model. Therefore, the company's ability to grow is dependent on our ability to foster and expand partner relationships — to this end, our 'customers' are the project partners we need to expand our production.

### 1.5 Marketing strategy. How do you plan on reaching your customers?

Engagement with professional networks and industry organizations, direct sales to prospective partners.
