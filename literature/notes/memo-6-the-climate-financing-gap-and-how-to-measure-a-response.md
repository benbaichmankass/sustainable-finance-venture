## What is already established

- LIT-040 (CPI 2024) closes the gap-magnitude question: adaptation finance reached USD 76bn in 2022 against modelled needs of USD 212bn/yr for 2024-2030 — about a third. 19% went to LDCs, 2% to SIDS, and 92% of tracked adaptation finance is public.
- The more useful finding is a documented absence. Flows are tracked at commitment level by intermediary, and private and domestic-budget adaptation finance is described as 'opaque', so NOBODY can say what share reaches households. The component's third question is a data gap, not an open question — and 92% public is itself the argument for a private poolable instrument.
- LC-26 (impact-evaluation method advances) is untouched and supplies the measurement half. Until it is read this memo has one leg.
