This memo is about the *mechanism*, not the impact. Group lending works, where it works, through information the lender does not have and sanctions the lender cannot apply. Whether that mechanism survives being financed from outside is the question the whole venture turns on, and it is not yet answered here.

## What is already established

- LC-02 is the load-bearing component and is furthest from done — 4 anchors of 12.
- LIT-022 (19th-century German rural credit cooperatives) is already the sharpest counterweight on file: the longest-running successful joint-liability institution financed its loans from LOCAL DEPOSITS. It was self-funded. The structure with the best track record did not take external wholesale capital into the group layer — which is exactly what this project proposes to do.
- That observation is currently sitting in MEMO-1 rather than here, and it belongs in both. It is also the intellectual origin of RQ-04's funding-source randomisation.
